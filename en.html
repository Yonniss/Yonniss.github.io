01. About me

I'm Yannis, a 17-year-old developer from L'Ampolla, a small coastal town near Tarragona, Spain. I move comfortably between software and hardware — from designing PCBs in KiCad to building complete web architectures for real businesses.

Currently in my 2nd year of High School (Science & Technology) while balancing freelance work and the development of IonicTech — my web digitalization project for local small businesses.

Native in Spanish, Catalan and Romanian, with B1 certified English. What attracts me most is that code and circuits end up solving real-world problems — not just on screens.

Outside of code you'll find me solving Rubik's cubes (sub-20 average on 3x3), playing chess (900 ELO) or working out at the gym. The pattern recognition from speedcubing and the tactical thinking from chess sneak into how I approach technical problems more than you'd think.
