Hi, I'm Yannis.

I study Computer Engineering at Universitat Rovira i Virgili and live in L'Ampolla, a small town in the Ebro Delta, Spain (yes, the one in the wallpaper).

I move between software and hardware: I build websites and apps, design PCBs in KiCad, program ESP32s and 3D print the enclosures. I freelance building websites for businesses, and I'm working on IonicTech, my project to bring small businesses online.

Away from the screen: Rubik's cube (sub-20 average on 3×3), chess (900 ELO and climbing) and loud rock, from classic to metal.

I speak Spanish, Catalan, Romanian and English, and I'm learning Japanese.

Open the Projects folder to see what I've built, or Mail if you'd like to talk.
